SignDuplex example collection — media credits

The collection contains 20 How2Sign-based examples and 5 generated everyday-activity clips. Event timelines are recorded model predictions.

Modifications: source sentence clips or existing Dev prefixes were normalized by the local demo. The five BARGE examples join two clips from the same original recording; some clips are trimmed at a resting boundary or end with a signing prefix. barge_4 includes a 0.8-second final-frame hold between turns. wait_2 omits the initial 0.667 seconds of its source; wait_5 extends an existing natural pause by 0.8 seconds using repeated hold frames. Event records were produced from these complete edited inputs. Packaging resizes the exact recorded-session input to 720-pixel width, re-encodes H.264/yuv420p, removes audio, and enables faststart without further frame additions, removals, or retiming.

English sentences are source annotations. No endorsement by the dataset authors is implied.

Background edit (2026-10-23): the 20 INCOMPLETE, WAIT, COMPLETE and BARGE examples have their green-screen backgrounds replaced with generated everyday indoor scenes. The original foreground, frame order and playback timing are retained. Recorded event predictions remain unchanged from the original videos; they were not recomputed after the background edit. The 5 REJECT clips are unchanged.


Generated everyday-activity clips

Files: reject-phone, reject-sleeve, reject-hair, reject-stretch, reject-papers.
These clips depict synthetic people and were generated locally with Wan2.2-T2V-A14B from text prompts. The model is distributed under Apache-2.0; model authors do not claim rights over generated content.
Official model and license information: https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-T2V-A14B#license-agreement

Processing: resized to 720 pixels wide, encoded as H.264/yuv420p at 30 fps with faststart. The published video is the same canonical input used to produce its event record.
